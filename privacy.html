Privacy policy

The developer collects no data through the Piano Confetti app. Your child can play offline without signing up.

Your child’s music and progress

The player name is optional. The app saves the name, avatar, settings, stars, stickers, recent songs and progress on your device. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Files and recordings

When a grown-up imports a MIDI file, the app saves a playable arrangement in its Documents folder. You can access that folder through Files or Finder. Depending on your settings, device backups may include those arrangements and other app data.

Record in the Piano room remembers the keys you press and when you press them. It never turns on the microphone. The take stays in memory until you start a new recording or leave the room.

Full Songbook purchases

Apple handles payment and restoration through your App Store account. Piano Confetti uses Apple’s purchase verification to unlock the extra songs and MIDI import.

Editing or deleting data

Open Grown-ups to edit or clear the player name, delete an imported song, or clear stars and stickers. Deleting the app removes its local data. Manage device backups separately in your Apple settings.

Support and beta feedback

If you email support or send feedback through TestFlight, the developer uses the details you send to answer your question or investigate the issue. Please leave your child’s name and other identifying details out of reports and screenshots.
