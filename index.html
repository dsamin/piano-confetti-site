How do we play our first song?

Turn your iPhone or iPad sideways. Choose a record sleeve without a padlock and tap Play. When a circle reaches the keyboard, press the lit key. The song waits while your child finds it. Hot Cross Buns is a good place to start because its melody uses just three keys.

To practice a few notes at a time, choose Learn. Listen to the phrase, then play the notes back when it’s your turn.

How do I change the speed or volume?

Tap the gear on Home. Read the three number words and tap their digits in order to open Grown-ups. Under Playing, choose a Song speed. Under Sound, set Loudest volume. If the piano is quiet, check your device volume and whether the sound is going to headphones or another speaker.

Which songs are free?

Eleven complete songs are included, among them Happy Birthday to You, London Bridge, Jingle Bells and Ode to Joy. Play and Learn are available for each. The seven instruments in Piano are free too. Full Songbook is an optional one-time purchase that adds the 22 padlocked songs and MIDI import.

How do I unlock or restore Full Songbook?

Tap a padlocked sleeve and choose Grown-ups. Pass the reading gate to see the price for your region. If the price doesn’t appear, check your internet connection and tap Retry App Store Price. If the app says purchases are restricted, a grown-up can check the device’s Screen Time purchase settings.

If you’ve already bought Full Songbook, use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Tap the gear on Home, pass the gate and tap Restore Purchases. Connect to the internet while restoring.

Can we play a tune from Files?

With Full Songbook, open Grown-ups and tap Choose a MIDI File. Select a MIDI file you have permission to use, then look for its arrangement in My Songs on Home. To remove an imported arrangement, open My Songs in Grown-ups and tap the trash button beside its title.

Where did our recording go?

In Piano, tap Record, play your tune and tap Stop. Tap Listen while you’re still in the room. The app remembers the notes and timing. Starting a new recording replaces the previous take, and leaving Piano clears it.

How do I manage my child’s data?

In Grown-ups, you can edit the player name and clear stars and stickers. Clearing progress keeps your settings and imported songs. Open Privacy policy in Grown-ups to read about local storage and device backups.
